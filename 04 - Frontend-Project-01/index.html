Meet the Outlaws

Arthur Morgan

A loyal outlaw and the heart of the Van der Linde gang, Arthur Morgan faces difficult choices while searching for purpose in a changing world.

John Marston

A determined gunslinger striving to protect his family, John Marston's journey becomes one of the most memorable stories in the Wild West.

Dutch van der Linde

The charismatic leader of the Van der Linde gang, Dutch inspires loyalty with his vision, but his ambitions slowly test the bonds of his family.

Sadie Adler

Fearless and independent, Sadie Adler transforms into one of the toughest survivors of the American frontier through courage and determination.
